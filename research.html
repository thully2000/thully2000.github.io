Research

My PhD research focuses on Behind-the-Meter Generation Forecasting for Grid Visibility. The objective is to improve visibility of ditributed energy resources connected to electricity networks and support more efficient network planning and operation.

Research Themes

Grid Visibility

Improving awareness of generation activity hidden from network operators.

Disaggregation

Developing disaggregation models for low voltage network monitoring data.

Sustainable Energy

Analysing the impact of sustainable resources on modern electricity systems.

Current Research Activities

  • Behind-the-meter solar generation estimation.
  • Disaggregation model development and validation.
  • Behavioural similarity incorporation into disaggregation.
  • Analysis of low-voltage monitoring and irradiance datasets.

Methods & Tools

Python, MATLAB, statistical modelling, energy system analysis, data analytics, and disaggregation methods.
